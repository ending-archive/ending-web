--- Day 3: The Ridge ---

Wind from the northwest, steady, perhaps twenty knots. Visibility good. Cloud base well above the ridge.

Observed two ravens working the updraft along the east face, tumbling for no purpose I could identify other than the obvious one. Noted, and then abandoned the notebook for ten minutes to watch simply because it seemed rude not to.

--- Day 7: The Marsh ---

Boots wet through by nine. Counted fourteen species of wading bird before noon and lost count of the insects entirely.

The marsh keeps no straight lines. Every path I take dissolves behind me, and I suspect the honest map of this place would be a verb, not a drawing.

--- Day 12: The Shore ---

The tide leaves its handwriting on the sand: ripples, castings, the neat punctuation of crab tracks. I copied what I could and let the sea correct the rest.

Final note: the edge is not a line. It is a conversation, held twice daily, and I am only a listener.
