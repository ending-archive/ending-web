--- Matins ---

Before the kettle,
before the light,
a small hour holds its breath.

Nothing is asked of you yet.
Nothing is owed.
The day has not found your name.

--- Midday ---

The sun stands straight overhead
and the shadows tuck themselves
beneath the feet of things.

Eat slowly.
Let the bread be bread.
Let the noon be long.

--- Vespers ---

The windows turn to mirrors.
The street lamps take their posts.
Somewhere a door closes gently,
the way a good day should.

Put down what you carried.
It will keep till morning.

--- Compline ---

Lay the hour down.
Lay the worry down.
Let the dark be soft,

and be, for these few breaths,
a small thing, warm and held,
held by a very large night.
