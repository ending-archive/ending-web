--- Source ---

The river begins as a rumour in the hills, a dampness in the moss that only the very attentive would call water. Nadia crouched over it with her sleeves rolled up, unsure whether she was witnessing a birth or an eavesdropping.

"This is where it starts," her guide said, and then, with a shrug that took in the whole hillside, "or somewhere near."

--- Bend ---

Downstream the river learns to turn. It meets stone and does not argue; it simply finds the long way round and calls it a path.

Nadia walked the bank for three days, notebook open, and wrote very little. The river was not the kind of subject that rewarded cleverness. It rewarded the willingness to be accompanied.

--- Mouth ---

At the delta the river forgot itself. Channels braided and unbraided, silt settled into new land, and the sea moved in to meet it with unhurried courtesy.

She sat on the last dry bank and watched fresh water and salt negotiate. Slow waters, her grandmother used to say, are the ones that arrive.
