How your data is handled when you use 3NDING.

3NDING does not track your reading habits, sell your data, or use advertising trackers. Your privacy is paramount.

Your reading bookmarks, favorites, and read-status are saved strictly inside your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device.
